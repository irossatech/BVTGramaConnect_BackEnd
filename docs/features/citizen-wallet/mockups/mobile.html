Citizen wallet: app screens

Draft mockups for BVTGC-131, for approval. Colours, fonts and shapes from the approved prototype (Mobile V2). Telugu is the default; switch to check both.

Notes for review

  • Every text is a draft. The points' name follows the default ("Grama points") until the client answers Q1 (BVTGC-144).
  • Errors from the server show under their field, in the citizen's language; long Telugu messages wrap to two lines.
  • Buttons, inputs and cards follow the prototype: 54 px pill buttons with a chevron, 12 to 16 px radii, Rubik headings, Nunito Sans body, Noto Sans Telugu at 88%.
  • Screens are drawn at 360 px wide, a budget phone's width.
