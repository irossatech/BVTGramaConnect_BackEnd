For Sprint 1 (Phase 1 release), the remaining risk sits in three places:
1. **The way production is actually run.** Production runs through the qa scripts on a hosting panel: 3 sync runserver/Gunicorn workers, a cron keep-alive and a hand-configured panel Nginx. 2. **Citizen registration with no throttle and no device attestation.** It relies on a client-sent `android_id` while Play Integrity is off, so post limits and blocks can be escaped. This is an accepted risk.
3. **No error tracking or alerting, and backups that may be unencrypted, on-host only, and without the keys needed to restore citizen identity.**

The two correctness bugs this audit found (the escalation stall, BE-DB-201, and the dashboard 500 from a public input, BE-ERR-201) are fixed.
